Umplia® for Cosmetics

Umplia for Cosmetics validates your Mandatory Health Notification (NSO) dossier before you file it. It checks that the documents are consistent with each other and that they comply with Decision 833 and the requirements of INVIMA, DIGEMID, ARCSA and AGEMED.

What it checks

Two validations across the whole dossier.

Internal validation
That every document says the same thing: qualitative and quantitative formula, label, specifications, stability and manufacturer declarations.
External validation
That the dossier complies with Decision 833 and its amendments, each health authority’s requirements, INCI nomenclature and the reference lists.

The report

What to fix, before you file.

Umplia never invents data. Anything it cannot read clearly is flagged as “Requires human review”, and the professional decides on every finding.

High impact
Fails a regulatory requirement: risk of rejection or an information request. The dossier is not ready to file while one exists.
Medium impact
A data point does not match within the dossier or a field is filled in wrong: an evaluator will question it.
Low impact
A writing or format defect that does not affect compliance or identification.
