Umplia® for Cosmetics

Umplia for Cosmetics validates your Mandatory Health Notification (NSO) dossier before you file it. It checks that the documents are consistent with each other and that they comply with Decision 833 and the requirements of INVIMA, DIGEMID, ARCSA and AGEMED.

New NSO

The full dossier for a new NSO, before you file.

2

Modifications

Every change to an NSO already granted, before you file. 19 modification types.

What it checks

Two validations across the whole dossier.

  • Internal validationThat every document says the same thing: qualitative and quantitative formula, label, specifications, stability and manufacturer declarations.
  • External validationThat the dossier complies with Decision 833 and its amendments, each health authority’s requirements, INCI nomenclature and the reference lists.

The report

What to fix, before you file.

  • High impactFails a regulatory requirement: risk of rejection or an information request. The dossier is not ready to file while one exists.
  • Medium impactA data point does not match within the dossier or a field is filled in wrong: an evaluator will question it.
  • Low impactA writing or format defect that does not affect compliance or identification.

Umplia never invents data. Anything it cannot read clearly is flagged as “Requires human review”, and the professional decides on every finding.

Who it is for

For anyone filing cosmetics in the Andean Community.

Regulatory affairs teams at cosmetic manufacturers, contract makers, importers, distributors and brands in Colombia, Peru, Ecuador and Bolivia. Also pharmaceutical companies that file cosmetic NSOs. Umplia for Cosmetics does not cover medicines registration.
