About

We want filing a dossier to stop being an act of faith.

Umplia, Inc. builds Umplia®, the platform that validates cosmetic regulatory dossiers before they are filed in Colombia, Peru, Ecuador and Bolivia.

Our story

Why Umplia exists.

Umplia was born from watching a regulatory affairs professional up close: hundreds of pages per dossier, reviewed by hand, one by one, knowing that a single inconsistent data point could become a request from the health authority.

That work demands rigor, and rigor shouldn’t depend on how many hours are left in the day. So we built Umplia: so regulatory teams spend their judgment deciding, not searching.

Purpose

Accelerate access to treatments that change and save lives.
