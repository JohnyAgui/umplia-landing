Our purpose

Accelerate access to treatments that change and save lives.

Umplia, Inc. builds Umplia®, the platform that validates cosmetic regulatory dossiers before they are filed in Colombia, Peru, Ecuador and Bolivia.

Our story

Rigor shouldn’t depend on the hours in the day.

Umplia was born from watching a regulatory affairs professional up close and seeing what was at stake in every review: a single inconsistent data point could become a request from the health authority.

So we built Umplia: so regulatory teams spend their judgment deciding, not searching.

Rigor
We review the whole file, document by document, not a sample.
Integrity
We never fill in what the file doesn’t say. Every decision belongs to the professional.
Speed
Minutes instead of hours, so your team gets to filing sooner.
