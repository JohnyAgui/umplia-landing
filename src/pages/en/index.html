The problem

Behind every cosmetic there is a dossier.

Hundreds of pages that a regulatory affairs professional reviews manually, one by one, 8 to 9 hours for each full new NSO dossier. Even so, some errors escape the human eye. The error that goes unnoticed today becomes a request from the health authority tomorrow.

The product

Umplia reviews your whole dossier before you file.

It reads every document in the file, validates that the information is consistent across the whole dossier and that it complies with Andean regulation, and gives you a report with findings ranked by impact. You decide on every finding.

Certainty first

Umplia never invents data.

When documents disagree, it shows which ones match and which one differs, and the professional decides. Anything it cannot read clearly is flagged as “Requires human review”.

Internal validation
Consistency across formula, label, specifications, stability and manufacturer declarations.
External validation
Decision 833 and its amendments, each authority’s requirements, INCI nomenclature and reference lists.
Findings by impact
High, medium and low, so you fix first what could hold up your filing.
