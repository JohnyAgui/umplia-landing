Personal data policy

In force since {{updated}}

This policy explains how Umplia, Inc. handles the personal data it receives through umplia.com, what it is used for and how you can exercise your rights, under Colombia’s Law 1581 of 2012 and its regulations. The Spanish version prevails.

Data controller

Umplia, Inc., the company that builds Umplia® for Cosmetics, operating in Bogotá, Colombia. Contact: {{mailto}}.

What we collect

  • Demo form: first name, last name, company, work email and mobile number, and your authorization to process them.
  • Request context: the page you write from, the language, the page where you entered the site, the referring site and the campaign (UTM) parameters of the link, if any.
  • Site measurement: we use Vercel Web Analytics, which counts visits in aggregate, without cookies and without identifying you.

We do not use cookies. To link your request to the campaign that brought you here, the site keeps those parameters in your browser’s session storage, which is cleared when you close the tab.

What we use it for

  • Answering your request and scheduling the demo.
  • Contacting you about Umplia in relation to your request.
  • Understanding, in aggregate, which pages and campaigns work best.

We do not sell your data.

Who else processes it

We use providers that process data on our behalf: Formspree, which receives the forms, and Vercel, which hosts the site and measures visits. Their servers may be outside Colombia, including in the United States. By giving your authorization, you accept this transfer for the purposes described.

Your rights

  • to know, update and correct your data;
  • to request proof of the authorization you gave us;
  • to know how we have used it;
  • to revoke the authorization or ask us to delete your data, unless a legal or contractual duty requires keeping it;
  • to access your data free of charge;
  • to file complaints with Colombia’s Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio.

How to exercise them

Write to {{mailto}} with your name, your request and a contact detail. We answer queries within 10 business days and claims within 15 business days, as set by articles 14 and 15 of Law 1581 of 2012.

How long we keep it

For as long as it is needed for the purposes described or while a legal duty requires it. After that, we delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page with its effective date.
